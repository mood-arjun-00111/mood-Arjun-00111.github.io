ℹ️ About MindShelf

A Place for Curious Minds

Built around one simple idea: reading should help you think.

MindShelf is a simple book-discovery website designed to help people find useful books without feeling overwhelmed by thousands of choices.

The featured collection focuses on money, investing, habits, psychology, business and personal development.

But the website isn't limited to those books. The Explore page connects to the Open Library search service so visitors can search for books beyond the featured collection.

A book is not valuable simply because it is popular. What matters is what you understand, question and apply after reading it.
